        Method and limits

        Corner detection

        The photo is shrunk to 360 px on its long side, converted to gray (Rec. 601 luma), blurred with a Gaussian (sigma 1.4) and run through a Sobel filter. Edges above an Otsu threshold are dilated, and everything reachable from the image border without crossing an edge is marked as background. The largest remaining region is one candidate; the largest region brighter than an Otsu split of the gray image is the other. For each, the convex hull of its outline is reduced to the largest-area four-corner shape, each side is refit as a least-squares line, and the corners are the line intersections. A candidate is accepted only when it fills most of its outline and the outline sits on strong edges. The corners are then refined at full resolution by searching along each side's normal for the strongest brightness step. When no candidate passes, the full image is used and the page is marked "Detection unsure".

        Flattening

        The four corners map to a rectangle through a 3 x 3 homography H, solved by the direct linear transform with h33 = 1: for each corner pair (x, y) to (u, v), u = (h11 x + h12 y + h13) / (h31 x + h32 y + 1) and the same for v, giving 8 linear equations in 8 unknowns. Points are normalized (centroid at the origin, mean distance sqrt 2) before solving. Each output pixel center is mapped back into the photo and sampled bilinearly. Output size is the longer of each pair of opposite sides, capped at 2,500 px on the long side.

        Filters

        • Grayscale: Rec. 601 luma, 0.299 R + 0.587 G + 0.114 B.
        • B&W document: Sauvola threshold T = m (1 + k (s / R - 1)), local mean m and standard deviation s over a square window about 1/16 of the short side, k = 0.2, R = 128, computed with integral images.
        • Enhanced color: the 1st and 99th percentile of luma are stretched to 0 and 255, with the same gain on R, G and B so colors keep their hue.

        Page sizes

        Letter is 612 x 792 pt, A4 is 595.28 x 841.89 pt (1 pt = 1/72 in, 1 in = 25.4 mm). The page turns landscape when the image is wider than tall. The image is scaled to fit inside the margins and centered. Fit to image makes the long side 792 pt plus margins.

        Limits

        • Each file is capped at 50 MB and each output at 300 pages.
        • Photos are processed at up to 2,500 px on the long side, about 210 dpi on a Letter page. Phone photos are larger, so the original resolution is not kept.
        • Detection expects one page that contrasts with what it lies on. Pages on a white desk, pages that run off the photo, curled or folded pages, and busy backgrounds can fail; drag the corners by hand. Page curl is not corrected.
        • There is no text recognition (OCR). Scanned PDFs are images and cannot be searched.
        • Encrypted or password-protected PDFs are refused. Fillable form fields are copied but not flattened. Links, bookmarks, document outlines, form actions and all scripts are dropped from outputs; form fields keep their printed appearance but are no longer fillable.
        • Typed initials use the built-in Helvetica font, which covers Western European characters only.
        • The drawn signature is a picture stamped on the page. It is not a cryptographic digital signature.
        • Compression re-encodes to JPEG. Transparent areas turn white, and photo metadata (including GPS location) is removed. A result larger than the original is flagged.
        • Files stay in this tab's memory and are gone when you close it. Nothing is stored or uploaded.
        • PDF libraries: pdf-lib 1.17.1 and pdf.js 3.11.174, loaded from jsDelivr with integrity hashes. If pdf.js cannot load, PDF tools still work without thumbnails.
